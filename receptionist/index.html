Questions people ask

Can I still use my normal WhatsApp?

Yes, on your personal number. The assistant needs its own number on WhatsApp's business platform, which can't also be used in the regular app. The number stays yours.

What if a customer needs to speak to me?

It tells them you'll be in touch and lets you know.

Do customers know it's automated?

Yes. It says it's an assistant in its first reply.

What calendar does it use?

Google Calendar. Use something else? Mention it when we chat.

What does it cost?

It depends on your business and how many messages you get. We'll quote you once we know your business.

What happens to my customers' information?

It's used only to answer and book. See the privacy policy.
