Leave it

Hold the treat in a closed hand. Wait for your dog to look away, then reward.

The problem

Clients forget the moves between sessions, and they can't practise what they can't remember.

The trainer visits clients at home and had no website or brand. We're building a private web app with her ten core beginner moves, each as a short clip with a few plain steps. Only clients who book sessions get access.

The approach

Calm, neutral and grown-up, with a forest green accent. It should feel like a professional service, because it is one. A web app rather than an app store listing, so clients open a link and start.

Landing page · Preview, 2026

A website for a WhatsApp receptionist

ProductHillo Receptionist (built under the working name Front Desk)
ForSmall businesses in George, Mossel Bay and surrounds
ScopePositioning, copy, design, front-end, motion
BuiltOne static page, no framework · hillo.co.za/receptionist
The brief

Explain a product nobody has seen before, to a busy owner skimming on their phone, in a few seconds.

The product answers a business's WhatsApp, books jobs into their calendar and speaks as the business, in English or Afrikaans. It was built as Front Desk and is now Hillo Receptionist. The big players in this space are global and self-serve. This one is local and done for you, so the page had to feel personal and trustworthy without faking it.

That ruled out the usual shortcuts: no stock photos, no logo walls, no star ratings and no invented numbers. The product had to explain itself.

Show it working

The conversation is the hero.

Instead of a headline about AI, the first thing you see is a real-feeling chat. A customer with a leaking geyser at 21:47 gets a safety tip, two time slots and a confirmed booking. Small captions under the messages show the work you can't see, like "Booked into your calendar".

Colour with rules

Every colour has one job.

WhatsApp green only ever means "this is WhatsApp", so it lives on customer bubbles and nowhere else. Tangerine is the one thing to do, so it's only on the main button. Everything else is a calm, deep blue. Every text colour passes accessibility contrast, including the small grey captions.

  • InkHeadings and body
  • True blueLinks, ticks, focus
  • TangerineMain button only
  • WhatsAppCustomer bubbles only
  • CanvasPage background
Local by default

Speaks English. Praat Afrikaans.

The same Saturday haircut enquiry, side by side in both languages. It's the page's one deep-blue band, so it lands as a moment rather than another feature.

Details

  • Motion reveals, never gates

    Chats type themselves out and headings come into focus as you scroll. Turn motion off and the whole page is still there, complete.

  • Honest by design

    Every chat is labelled as an example, and the assistant says it's automated in its first reply.

  • Light enough for rural data

    One small file with no framework, so it loads fast on a weak signal.

  • Three ways in, no more

    WhatsApp first, a short form as backup. Not a call to action in every section.
